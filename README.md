# Cape Retail Co. - Retail Sales Analysis (2025)

## Project Overview
This project analyses 240 fictional retail transactions from a South African
retail company, Cape Retail Co., to identify sales trends, regional performance, 
product performance, and estimated gross profitability.

The project demonstrates practical skills in data cleaning, financial calculations, 
Excel Pivot-tables, data visualisation, and business reporting.

## Business Objectives
* Identify the highest-performing sales regions.
* Determine which products generate the most revenue.
* Analyse monthly sales performance.
* Compare net revenue and estimated gross profit across regions.
* Identify and document data-quality issues.

## Tools and Skills
Microsoft Excel: Data cleaning, structured tables, formulas, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and KPI reporting.

SQL: Sales analysis and aggregation (planned next stage).

## Data Cleaning
Six data-quality issues were identified, including inconsistent text formatting, 
a date-format issue and missing transaction values.

The original dataset was preserved, and changes were documented in a cleaning log. 
Missing financial values were not replaced with unsupported estimates.

## Key Findings
* Regional Performance: Gauteng generated the highest recorded net revenue with R933 935,00 (32,51% of total net revenue).
* Product Performance: Laptops were the leading product by net revenue with R1 393 750,00 (48,52% of total net revenue).
* Monthly Performance: December recorded the highest monthly net revenue with R355 125.50 accounting for 12.36% of net revenue.
* Profitability: Gauteng also generated the highest estimated gross profit margin of 27%.
* Data Quality: Two transactions contained unresolved missing financial information,
  limiting the completeness of financial totals

## Limitations
The dataset is fictional and covers only 2025. Estimated gross profit excludes operating expenses, 
and incomplete transactions limit the financial analysis.
